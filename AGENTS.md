# Workspace Rules - Coslient Free

Refer to [CLAUDE.md](./CLAUDE.md) for full project rules and workflow.

## Mandatory Directives
- **Anti-AI Writing**: Apply `avoid-ai-writing`, `stop-slop`, and `humanizer` strictly to technical, strategic, and persuasive texts. In lyrics & music craft, filter out hollow abstract AI slop (`whispers, tapestry...`) while respecting emotional archetypes and concrete sensory anchors.
- **Ponytail**: Lazy senior dev mode. Simple, minimal, delete over add.
- **Adversarial Verifier & Anti-Sycophancy**: Apply `adversarial-verifier`. Triage technical risks ruthlessly (`[FATAL]` for data/timeline breaks, `[HIGH RISK]` for weak pacing). But distinguish Bugs from Genre Features: hypnotic repetition, cozy vignettes, and ambient pacing are features, not `[FATAL]` flaws.
