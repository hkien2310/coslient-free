```text
An old grocery box, a roll of masking tape, and a crooked piece of driftwood found by the rocks. That was all it took to build a kingdom for the afternoon.

When we are young, we want to conquer the whole ocean before dinner. When our hair turns silver, we are happy to kick off our canvas shoes, sit in a folding chair on the cliff, and wear the crooked cardboard crown our grandkids made for us.

By five o'clock, the yellow sneakers and the helmet were left on the warm stone steps so two pairs of bare feet could wade into the tide before heading home for supper.

What was your favorite homemade toy or backyard game when you were small? 👑🌊
```
