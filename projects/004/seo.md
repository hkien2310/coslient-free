## MÔ TẢ YOUTUBE (DESCRIPTION — LETTER FORMAT v5.0)

Hello, my dear friends,

After publishing our last visual piece, I spent several quiet evenings reading through your notes and letters. So many of you wrote about that heavy, unspoken weight we carry for years—holding up skies that never asked for our sacrifice, wearing heavy iron armor just to protect ourselves from falling.

A few weeks ago, while sitting beside an open window watching the sea wind catch the heavy linen fabric of an old curtain, a simple truth settled in my heart. We spend so much energy holding on, measuring every breath for someone else's sake, forgetting that hands were never made to stay so full. We freeze ourselves inside quiet stone walls just to survive, when all our souls really long for is to step onto the open stone, let the heavy hem sweep the dust away, and dance until the weight is finally gone.

In this piece, "Unbound," I wanted to build a world out of tactile felted wool, weathered cedar wood, polymer clay, and real ocean water—a sanctuary where every character finds the courage to set their heavy armor down by the sea wall and step barefoot into the wind.

I hope these warm cello notes and wooden percussion beats bring a breath of quiet space into your day. May it remind you that you don't owe the storm another word, and that fear ends the exact moment you allow yourself to let go.

🎬 About This Film

"Unbound" is a visual and musical meditation on emotional release, healing, and self-liberation. Designed using handcrafted tactile diorama stop-motion aesthetics, the film weaves together stories of artisans, wanderers, gardeners, and dreamers who discover the quiet joy of setting down their burdens. Dedicated to anyone who feels tired of holding everything together—you are allowed to breathe, to rest, and to feel weightless at last.
⸻

🎬 How This Film Was Made

Every story, song, lyric, visual direction, and edit was created by me using AI as a creative instrument under human artistic direction.

• Midjourney v6.1 / Nano Banana Pro — visual handcrafted tactile diorama generation
• Runway Gen-3 / Veo 3 — gentle fluid tactile motion animation
• Suno — original percussive chamber pop music created from my own lyrics and musical direction

AI was the instrument, but the heart behind it is human.
