## MÔ TẢ

Hello, my dear friends,

After publishing our last visual piece, I spent several evenings reading through your notes and quiet thoughts. So many of you spoke about that subtle, heavy weight of spending years trying to fit into shapes that were never designed for your soul.

A few weeks ago, while sitting at an old wooden drafting table watching sheets of plain paper being pressed into rigid iron trays, a simple thought wouldn’t leave me. We spend half our lives smoothing down our sharp corners, hiding our creases, and folding our wings just to pass strangers' tests. But paper was never meant to remain flat—it was created to hold form, to bend, to dance, and to catch the light.

In this film, "Paper Creases," you will follow a tall, faceless papercraft dancer crafted from raw matte paper and porcelain. Through hand-sculpted paper dioramas—from quiet lotus ponds and clockwork gears to sunburst arches—she steps out of the folded constraints, discovering gold leaf seams beneath every tear. Accompanying her journey is an original rhythmic chamber pop piece featuring felt piano, pizzicato cello, and tactile paper snap percussion at 98 BPM.

I hope this film leaves you with a quiet reminder to breathe, to honor every crease you've earned, and to step out into your own rhythm today. Thank you for being here with me.
