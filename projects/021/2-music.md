# ==========================================
# 🍂 TRACK 1: LOOK AT THEM, DARLIN' (~4:20)
# ==========================================

## 🔍 1. Ý ĐỒ
Hai vợ chồng người hát đi dạo công viên chiều thu, người chồng huých nhẹ tay vợ bảo nhìn hai cụ già đang chơi xích đu đùa nghịch như đôi trẻ. Cả hai cùng mỉm cười ấm áp và người chồng trêu vợ: *"40 năm nữa chắc chắn em cũng bắt anh đẩy xích đu như thế này."*  
**Nhạc cụ dẫn dắt (Lead):** Fingerpicked acoustic guitar lead, soft pedal steel swells, gentle warm fiddle.  
**Năng lượng:** Êm đềm, gảy ngón mộc mạc, mở đầu ấm áp (68 BPM).

### 🎣 BẢN ĐỒ HOOK TRACK 1:
* **Mini-hook Mở đầu (0:10):** `[Smiling whisper]` *"Hey look at them, darlin'..."*
* **Mini-hook Biểu cảm:** `[Chuckle]` ở Verse 3 & Verse 5, `[Pause]` ở Bridge.
* **Mini-hook Nhạc cụ:** Câu lướt fiddle ngọt ngào ở `[Interlude]`.
* **Hook chính (Điệp khúc):** *"Watching him push that wooden swing, she's twenty years old again."*

## 🏷️ 2. TÊN BÀI HÁT
👉 **Look at Them, Darlin'**

## 🎛️ 3. SUNO v6 STYLE PROMPT
```text
Americana, classic country-folk, acoustic singer-songwriter. Unhurried gentle pocket, 68 BPM. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar lead, subtle upright bass, soft pedal steel swells, warm fiddle. Warm lived-in baritone, smiling conversational delivery, clear diction. Gentle verses swelling into a warm comforting chorus. Warm analog tape room, dry acoustic space.
```

## 📜 4. SUNO PERFORMANCE LYRICS
```text
# Look at Them, Darlin'

[Extended Intro | Fingerpicked acoustic guitar lead, soft fiddle swells, warm tape hiss, slow unhurried pace]

[Verse 1]
[Intimate and soft]
The October wind was blowing dry,
the dry leaves rustled on the ground.
I pulled you close against my coat,
without another soul around.

[Verse 2]
[Smiling whisper]
"Hey look at them, darlin'," I whispered low,
and nudged your elbow with my sleeve.
An old man by that wooden swing,
with gray hair blowing in the leaves.

[Chorus]
[Warm and conversational]
Time took the brown out of his hair,
and bent the fingers on her hands...
Watching him push that wooden swing,
she's twenty years old again.

[Interlude | Gentle guitar picking, sweet fiddle lick, pedal steel breathing space]

[Verse 3]
She kicked her boots toward the sky,
and laughed the way she must have then.
[Chuckle]
He caught the chains to slow her down,
and make her laugh like that again.

[Verse 4]
He pulled warm chestnuts from his coat,
and cracked the paper in the breeze.
[Tenderly]
You squeezed my hand and softly smiled,
watching her boots against his knees.

[Verse 5]
She stood up slow and smoothed her coat,
and brushed the dry leaves from her hem.
[Chuckle]
He offered her his crooked arm,
like a ballroom dancer back in nineteen-ten.

[Pre-Chorus]
[Tenderly]
You don't need an old photograph,
to see what never fades away...

[Chorus]
Time took the brown out of his hair,
and bent the fingers on her hands...
Watching him push that wooden swing,
she's twenty years old again.

[Acoustic Guitar Solo | Fingerpicked acoustic melody, subtle upright bass]

[Warm Fiddle Solo | Emotional soaring fiddle, gentle pedal steel swells]

[Bridge]
[Intimate and reflective]
I've spent my years in such a rush,
afraid of what the clock might take...
Wondering how a young man's heart
could bend without the strength to break.
[Pause]
I leaned in close beside your ear,
and told you what I know is true:
"Forty years from now, my love,
that's gonna be me and you."

[Final Chorus]
[Soaring warm harmonies]
Time took the brown out of his hair,
and bent the fingers on her hands...
Watching him push that wooden swing,
she's twenty years old again.
[Tenderly]
Yes, watching him push that wooden swing,
she's twenty years old again.

[Outro | Lingering acoustic guitar, autumn wind, soft humming]
[Soft Humming]
Mm-mm...
Rusty chains humming in the breeze...
Two lovers underneath the trees.
Forty years from now, my love,
just as young as these.

[Fade Out]
```

# ==========================================
# 🏡 TRACK 2: YOUR TURN ON THE SWING (~4:20)
# ==========================================

## 🔍 1. Ý ĐỒ
Hai cụ già rời đi, người chồng kéo tay vợ lại chiếc xích đu gỗ, ấn nhẹ vai bảo vợ ngồi xuống. Người vợ ngượng ngùng rồi cũng bật cười khúc khích khi bay lên không trung. Hai người dắt tay nhau qua chiếc cầu gỗ nhỏ về dưới ánh đèn đường với lời hứa trọn đời: *"Bốn mươi năm nữa em vẫn mãi là cô gái hai mươi tuổi của anh."*  
**Nhạc cụ dẫn dắt (Lead):** Warm acoustic guitar strumming lead, singing fiddle, soft pedal steel, subtle upright bass (CÙNG DÀN NHẠC).  
**Năng lượng:** Tăng tiến từ 68 lên 72 BPM, ấm sực, hạnh phúc, rạng rỡ.

### 🎣 BẢN ĐỒ HOOK TRACK 2:
* **Mini-hook Mở đầu (0:10):** `[Smiling whisper]` *"The old folks walked into the dusk..."*
* **Mini-hook Biểu cảm:** `[Chuckle]` ở Verse 2, `[Spoken Word]` ở Verse 4.
* **Mini-hook Nhạc cụ:** Tiếng quạt chả guitar ấm kết hợp câu fiddle ở `[Interlude]`.
* **Hook chính (Điệp khúc):** *"Whenever you're beside me here, you're twenty years old right now."*

## 🏷️ 2. TÊN BÀI HÁT
👉 **Your Turn on the Swing**

## 🎛️ 3. SUNO v6 STYLE PROMPT
```text
Americana, classic country-folk, acoustic singer-songwriter. Steady unhurried walking pocket, warm rhythmic strum, 72 BPM. Acoustic guitar strumming lead, warm singing fiddle, soft pedal steel swells, subtle upright bass. Warm lived-in baritone, smiling conversational delivery, clear diction. Gentle verses building into an uplifting tender chorus. Warm analog tape room, dry acoustic space.
```

## 📜 4. SUNO PERFORMANCE LYRICS
```text
# Your Turn on the Swing

[Extended Intro | Warm acoustic guitar strumming, soaring gentle fiddle, steady unhurried walking tempo]

[Verse 1]
[Smiling whisper]
The old folks walked into the dusk,
the swing was swaying in the air.
I took you gently by the hand,
and led you toward that wooden chair.

[Verse 2]
[Chuckle]
You laughed and said, "Now don't you start,
we're way too old for playground games!"
[Tenderly]
I gently nudged you to sit down,
and held the cold and rusty chains.

[Chorus]
[Warm and uplifting lift]
Don't worry 'bout the passing years,
or silver threads upon your brow...
Whenever you're upon this swing,
you're twenty years old right now.

[Interlude | Warm acoustic guitar strum, singing fiddle lick, soft pedal steel]

[Verse 3]
I gave your back a gentle shove,
your boots went flying in the breeze.
[Smiling]
You let out such a joyful laugh,
and kicked the gold leaves from the trees.

[Verse 4]
We crossed the little wooden bridge,
and watched the crescent moon rise high.
[Tenderly]
I wrapped your red scarf round your neck,
beneath that dark October sky.

[Verse 5]
We walked on home beneath the lamps,
our fingers tangled in my coat.
[Spoken Word]
You leaned your head against my arm,
and hummed a sweet familiar note.

[Pre-Chorus]
[Smiling]
The world can keep on rushing by,
we've got no other place to be...

[Chorus]
Don't worry 'bout the passing years,
or silver threads upon your brow...
Whenever you're upon this swing,
you're twenty years old right now.

[Acoustic Guitar Solo | Warm rhythmic picking, steady comforting groove]

[Soaring Fiddle Solo | Singing warm fiddle, emotional pedal steel swells]

[Bridge]
[Intimate and tender]
People chase a thousand dreams,
and fear the day they're growing old...
They count the wrinkles on their face,
and miss the greatest story told.
[Pause]
Holding your hand along this street,
with nothing left to prove or hide...
I've got the richest kind of life,
with you right by my side.

[Final Chorus]
[Soaring bright warm harmonies]
Don't worry 'bout the passing years,
or silver threads upon your brow...
Whenever you're beside me here,
you're twenty years old right now.
[Joyfully]
Yes, whenever you're beside me here,
you're twenty years old right now.

[Outro | Acoustic guitar strumming, gentle fiddle fade, footsteps on stone]
[Soft Humming]
Mm-mm...
A yellow porch light in the cold...
The sweetest story ever told.
With you, my love, beside me here,
we never will grow old.

[Fade Out]
```
