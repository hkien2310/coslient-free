The morning was warm on the stone of the street
The baker laid out all his berry tarts sweet
Then gray summer clouds rolled in heavy and low
And nobody hurried or had where to go

Oh come find a shelter come in from the rain
The big green awning reaches down the lane
Pull up a chair there is room here for all
With a kettle of tea by the cedar wood wall

The baker sliced open a melon so red
Two small paper boats drifted past by the shed
An old mandolin started playing a tune
To welcome the rain in the cool afternoon

At four in the evening the downpour grew still
A yellow round lantern was lit on the sill

Oh here is the shelter that welcomes the night
Where every small window is glowing with light
Let the summer rain drum on the roof overhead
The door was left open the table was spread

The cat is asleep on the cedar wood chair
Cool night rain in the summer air
There is shelter right here
