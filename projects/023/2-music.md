# 023: Hội Chợ Mùa Thu Ven Sông (The Riverside Autumn Jubilee)

Hai bài hát phong cách Americana / Porch Folk mộc ấm áp, giàu chất thơ, tinh nghịch và tràn ngập sinh khí tự do. Mạch chuyện xuyên suốt về một đôi vợ chồng già cùng nhau đi phiên chợ mùa thu ven sông: sáng dạo phố ngắm bạn bè lối xóm rộn rã (4/4 nảy nhịp), chiều dắt tay nhau khiêu vũ dưới ánh trăng non (3/4 waltz). Đập tan hoàn toàn các công thức văn mẫu AI, từ ngữ đời thường dân dã, không gian mộc mạc không có banjo, kéo dài trên 4 phút mỗi bài.

---

## Track 1: The Morning Jubilee

Buổi sáng rực rỡ tại hội chợ phiên ven sông: đôi vợ chồng già thả bộ ra bến thuyền ngắm thuyền chở bí ngô cập bến, sạp cúc vàng, quầy bánh nướng thơm lừng quế nồng, bàn xoay gốm của Clara và dăm bào gỗ thông bay trong nắng sớm.

### Style prompt
```text
Americana acoustic porch folk, unhurried rolling 76 BPM in a major key, warm analog tape-room sound with dry acoustic space, fingerpicked acoustic guitar lead, singing fiddle lines, subtle warm pedal steel accents, steady upright bass, lived-in female alto with joyful conversational delivery and clear diction; sunny front-porch storytelling swelling into an uplifting chorus.
```

### Exclude
```text
banjo, autotune, synths, electronic drums, vocal ad-libs, oohs
```

### Lyrics
```text
[Intro | Upbeat acoustic guitar picking, sweet fiddle lead, steady acoustic groove]

[Verse 1 | Warm conversational alto, joyful strumming, steady upright bass]
Mist is lifting off the water,
first wooden boat bumps on the pier.
Heaped right up with yellow pumpkins
and the biggest apples of the year.
Henry down at his workbench,
shaving curls of white pine thin,
Blows the sawdust off his knuckles
with a broad and easy grin.

[Pre-Chorus | Soft pedal steel accents, fiddle enters with a bounce]
Take my arm and watch your footing,
smell the baker's cinnamon sweet.
Old wood carts with yellow daisies
parked along the cobblestone street.

[Chorus | Full acoustic roll, pedal steel and fiddle soaring, wooden foot taps]
Don't you leave your Sunday jacket
hanging on the closet door.
Put your bright red sweater on
and walk along the river shore.
We spent too long staying home,
let the river run on by,
Come on down to the jubilee
underneath this morning sky!

[Interlude | Playful fiddle and acoustic guitar breakdown, rhythmic wooden foot taps]

[Verse 2 | Warm storytelling, acoustic guitar and upright bass]
Clara sits behind her pottery wheel,
grey hair twisted in a knot.
Spinning wet clay through her fingers,
turning mud into a honey pot.
Arthur brings an apple turnover,
steaming hot right off the tray.
Says, "Clara, wipe your apron off,
take a bite and come and play!"

[Pre-Chorus | Acoustic strum picks up speed, fiddle rings clear]
Neighbors in their heavy flannels
trading loaves and garden greens.
You took a warm crust off the tray,
best thing that you ever seen.

[Chorus | Full acoustic roll, joyous and wide]
Don't you leave your Sunday jacket
hanging on the closet door.
Put your bright red sweater on
and walk along the river shore.
We spent too long staying home,
let the river run on by,
Come on down to the jubilee
underneath this morning sky!

[Verse 3 | Intimate vocal, relaxed fingerpicking]
Sparrows hopping on the stones,
pecking crusts beside my shoe.
You push your old cap back a bit
and I lean my arm on you.
Every grey hair in the sunshine,
every laugh line had to earn,
We’ve got all day on our boots
and we got no place to return.

[Bridge | Slower warm acoustic strum, soaring fiddle swells]
Take that heavy woolen muffler off,
drape it right across the pine.
Feel the warm sun on your shoulders
as you put your hand in mine.
Let the fiddle take the tune now,
let the guitars start to play,
There’s a whole lot of living left
down along the dock today!

[Solo | Extended soaring fiddle solo trading joyful licks with acoustic guitar]

[Chorus | Grand full band lift, resonant, triumphant, joyous]
Don't you leave your Sunday jacket
hanging on the closet door.
Put your bright red sweater on
and walk along the river shore.
We spent too long staying home,
let the river run on by,
Come on down to the jubilee
underneath this morning sky!

[Outro | Fiddle and acoustic guitar trading gentle licks, quiet laughter, fading with the river breeze]
Down along the river shore.
(We spent too long staying home...)
Shake that red sweater loose.
Underneath the morning sky.
```

## Track 2: Dance Like Nobody's Young

Hoàng hôn buông trên sàn gỗ ven sông: chợ vãn, các sạp hàng dọn đồ lên xe bán tải. Người chồng tháo cà vạt vắt lên cọc gỗ, kéo tay người vợ ra sàn mùn cưa thông khiêu vũ theo điệu van-xơ 3/4 mộc mạc, ngọt ngào cùng ban nhạc sồi già.

### Style prompt
```text
Americana acoustic folk waltz, gentle unhurried 3/4 time 68 BPM in a major key, warm analog tape-room sound with dry acoustic space, fingerpicked acoustic guitar lead, singing fiddle melodies, subtle warm pedal steel swells, warm upright bass, lived-in female alto with tender conversational delivery and clear diction; twilight front-porch storytelling swaying into a warm uplifting chorus.
```

### Exclude
```text
banjo, autotune, synths, electronic drums, vocal ad-libs, oohs
```

### Lyrics
```text
[Intro | Gentle acoustic guitar 3/4 fingerpicking, soft pedal steel swell, sweet fiddle melody]

[Verse 1 | Warm conversational alto, gentle 3/4 waltz pocket, acoustic guitar and bass]
Sun is slipping in the willows,
water turning orange and grey.
Paper lanterns down the railing
lighting up the end of day.
Miller sets his cider cup down,
draws the bow across the string.
Folks are packing up the benches,
waiting for the tune to sing.

[Pre-Chorus | Pedal steel swells gently, fiddle enters with a warm sway]
Crates are loaded on the pickup,
shadows leaning on the sand.
You walk right up through the clover,
red fall leaf inside your hand.

[Chorus | Full warm acoustic lift, 3/4 waltz swell, fiddle soaring sweet and steady]
Let the young folks go running down the road,
let them race until they tire!
We’ll just take this wooden floor
and warm our hands beside the fire.
Two old boots in the pine sawdust,
holding on and stepping light,
We’re dancing like nobody’s
young tonight!

[Interlude | Sweet fiddle and acoustic guitar waltz, gentle swaying tempo]

[Verse 2 | Intimate storytelling, relaxed acoustic guitar and upright bass]
You don't ask me if I want to,
no, you never ask at all.
Just reach out and take my fingers,
pull me right off of the wall.
You took off that tight old necktie,
hung it right across the post.
You’re the one I picked for dancing,
the one that I still love the most.

[Pre-Chorus | Sweet fiddle builds, upright bass walks warmly in 3/4]
You step right upon my shoe sole,
then you just look down and grin.
Say you lost your count of three,
so you just turn us round again.

[Chorus | Full warm acoustic lift, 3/4 waltz swell, fiddle soaring sweet and steady]
Let the young folks go running down the road,
let them race until they tire!
We’ll just take this wooden floor
and warm our hands beside the fire.
Two old boots in the pine sawdust,
holding on and stepping light,
We’re dancing like nobody’s
young tonight!

[Verse 3 | Reflective, smiling vocal, gentle 3/4 guitar picking]
Look at all them little crow's feet
crinkling round your grey-green eyes.
Every winter that we made it through,
none of it told any lies.
Still that same look in your face,
hasn't changed a single bit,
Like a stove pipe in the cabin
when the evening wood is lit.

[Bridge | Slower, tender acoustic strum, warm fiddle swells, evening intimacy]
Water's splashing on the dock pilings,
stars are coming through the blue.
You pull me in by the elbow,
I smell the cider on you too.
Let the night come down the valley,
let the breeze begin to blow,
We got plenty of wood under our feet
and nowhere we have to go.

[Solo | Extended soaring fiddle waltz solo trading soulful, tender licks with acoustic guitar and pedal steel]

[Chorus | Grand full band lift, resonant, triumphant, joyous 3/4 waltz]
Let the young folks go running down the road,
let them race until they tire!
We’ll just take this wooden floor
and warm our hands beside the fire.
Two old boots in the pine sawdust,
holding on and stepping light,
We’re dancing like nobody’s
young tonight!

[Outro | Gentle guitar picking, fiddle and pedal steel fading into river breeze, quiet laughter]
Dancing like nobody's young tonight...
(Nowhere we have to go...)
One more turn before we leave.
Down along the river shore.
```
