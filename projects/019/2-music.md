# PROJECT 019: WE DID IT
**Single Master Track Specification & Production Script**  
*Thư mục: `projects/019/2-music.md` | Coslient Free*

---

## 🔍 1. PHÂN TÍCH

**Tứ thơ:** Hai vợ chồng già ngồi hiên nhà, nhìn lũ con đã lớn cười nói ngoài sân. Quay sang nhau — "We did it." Nhớ lại hồi xe hỏng giữa đường, hồi ăn cơm trắng cả tháng, hồi con ốm thức trắng đêm. Tất cả đã qua. Lũ trẻ thành người rồi.

**Cảm xúc:** Tự hào, hạnh phúc, xúc động — không bi lụy, không dạy đời.

**Không gian:** Hiên nhà buổi tối, tiếng trẻ con ngoài sân, dế kêu, ánh đèn vàng.

**Mỏ neo:** Đôi giày mòn đế của con, bao gạo kéo dài cả tháng, căn nhà im ắng khi đứa cuối dọn đi.

---

## 🏷️ 2. TÊN BÀI HÁT

👉 **We Did It**

## 🎛️ 3. SUNO STYLE PROMPT

```text
Acoustic folk, warm porch-recording intimacy, gentle fingerpicked guitar building to confident strumming, soft fiddle swells, light brushed snare, warm male vocal with lived-in storytelling delivery and female harmony joining choruses, heartfelt steady 88 BPM
```

---

## 📜 4. SUNO PERFORMANCE LYRICS

```text
# We Did It

[Intro | Gentle fingerpicked acoustic guitar, evening porch sounds, soft cricket ambience]

[Verse 1 | Warm storytelling vocal, fingerpicked guitar, light bass]
The porch light's on and the yard is loud tonight
Our girl is laughing hard the way she did at five
The boy is carrying his daughter way up high
She pulls his ear and points up at the sky

[Chorus 1 | Guitar opens to strumming, fiddle enters, female harmony joins]
We did it, me and you
Every long night, every worn-out shoe
We did it, hand in hand
Not the way we ever planned
But we did it

[Verse 2 | Intimate vocal, guitar picks gently, bass walks in]
The truck broke down that winter, we were twenty-three
You walked home through the rain, arms full of groceries
We made one bag of rice last half of January
And every night you made it taste like something new

[Chorus 2 | Fuller arrangement, harmonies richer, brushed snare joins]
We did it, me and you
Every long night, every worn-out shoe
We did it, hand in hand
Not the way we ever planned
But we did it

[Bridge | Stripped to vocal and single guitar, tender and quiet]
The house went quiet when the last one packed the car
I found your hand across the bed and asked now what
You said I guess we learn to sleep past six again
And we just lay there smiling in the dark

[Final Chorus | Full warmth, fiddle soars gently, both voices together]
We did it, me and you
Every long night, every worn-out shoe
We did it, look at them
I would do it all again

[Outro | Guitar fingerpicking slows, porch sounds return, last fiddle note fades]
The yard is getting still, the cars pull down the lane
You lean into my arm the way you always do
```
