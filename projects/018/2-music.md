```text
# A Place Back Home

[Intro | Bouncing fingerpicked acoustic guitar, lively foot stomp and handclaps, joyful acoustic harmonica riff]

[Verse 1 | Warm energetic vocal, bouncing acoustic rhythm, walking upright bass]
I wake right up with a tune in my head
I tune the strings and I jump out of bed
I think of you and the joy that you gave
A big warm smile and a friendly wave

[Chorus 1 | Driving upbeat folk groove, rich vocal harmonies, celebratory harmonica hook]
Hey, my friend, on any road you roam
You have always got a place back home

[Interlude | Playful piano riffs, rhythmic foot stomps, bouncy harmonica lead]

The street is loud and the sun is high

[Chorus 2 | Full energetic folk drive, soaring harmonies, infectious rhythm]
Hey, my friend, on any road you roam
You have always got a place back home
The water is hot and the room is wide
So kick off your boots and step inside

[Bridge | Strip down to driving acoustic guitar and heartfelt energetic vocal]
You do not owe me a single dime
Just loving my song was the sweetest time
I send this beat on the rolling wind
To say I am glad that you were my friend

[Solo | Soulful soaring acoustic harmonica solo and rhythmic piano over driving bass]

[Final Chorus | Joyful acoustic peak, singing together, full stomping groove]
Hey, my friend, on any road you roam
You have always got a place back home
```
