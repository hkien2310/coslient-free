# 025: Những Chỗ Ngồi Để Dành Bên Bờ Vịnh

Hai bài hát thuộc **Chế độ 2 — Ý niệm / Đa diện (`Reflective & Multi-Faceted Perspective`)**, thiết kế theo kiến trúc **`Two-Track Sanctuary Arc`** và quy chuẩn **Độ uyển chuyển, Liên kết mềm & Mở rộng nửa sau khổ hát (`Lyrical Fluidity, Flowing Transitions & Second-Half Verse Expansion`)** tại [`craft/music_craft.md`](../../craft/music_craft.md):
- **Track 1 (`Keep a Seat by the Sea` — D Major, 76 BPM):** Nhịp bước buổi sớm mai khoáng đạt bên vách đá biển (`gentle coastal walking pulse`). Toàn bộ các khổ `Verse` gieo vần chéo toàn phần (`ABAB CDCD` — 100% các dòng đều có vần chuẩn xác) kết hợp điệp cấu trúc mở rộng ở 4 dòng cuối, dẫn vào `Chorus` 6 dòng gieo vần đôi tức thì (`AABBAA`: `me/free - bay/day - me/sea`).
- **Track 2 (`Where the Evening Water Shines` — D Major với hòa âm bậc IV G Major ấm áp, 72 BPM):** Bản Song ca Nam – Nữ đối thoại dịu dàng lúc hoàng hôn buông trên mặt vịnh (`warm evening sway`, giảm nhẹ 4 BPM từ 76 xuống 72 BPM và giữ chung trục giọng D Major với Track 1). Toàn bộ `Verse` và `Bridge` gieo vần chéo toàn phần (`ABAB CDCD`), `Pre-Chorus` gieo vần đôi (`AABB`) và `Chorus` 6 dòng gieo vần đôi liền mạch (`AABBCC`: `bay/away - pier/clear - pines/shines`).

## Track 1: Keep a Seat by the Sea

Buổi sáng trong trẻo trên thị trấn vách đá ven vịnh biển: qua từng góc bờ vịnh độc lập, những người già dậy sớm lau khô lớp sương trên băng ghế gỗ nhìn ra đại dương cho bước chân người đi bộ lên dốc, kê sẵn hai chiếc ghế bên lan can cầu tàu và rót cà phê nóng ra cốc sứ đón người bạn già dưới bầu trời hải âu chao liệng.

### Style prompt
```text
Warm coastal Americana acoustic folk in D major, 76 BPM with a gentle morning walking pulse and open-air feel. Flesh-fingerpicked acoustic guitar with natural timing variations leads alongside soft strummed layers. Warm woody upright bass sits slightly behind the beat over a light felt kick, soft shaker, and brushed snare maintaining a steady, grounded flow. Lyrical solo violin and light mandolin phrases answer the vocal lines, joined by gentle human whistling in the intro and outro. Mature weathered male baritone sings with warm close-mic melodic intimacy, joined by soft female third harmonies in the choruses. Warm low-mid focused analog production with rolled-off gentle highs.
```

### Exclude
```text
EDM, glossy pop, aggressive drums, autotune, synths, harsh high frequencies
```

### Lyrics
```text
[Intro: Fingerpicked guitar and gentle whistling]

[Verse 1: Warm male vocal, close-mic]
There's a golden light across the bay
as the morning tide rolls in,
and the breeze comes up the cliffside path
where the quiet days begin.
Someone wiped the dew from the wooden bench
looking out across the sea,
for every step that climbs the hill,
for every heart that wanders free.

[Chorus: Warm male lead, soft female harmony]
Keep a seat by the sea for me,
where the morning wind blows free,
where the white sails drift across the bay
at the breaking of the day.
There's an open place for you and me
when you keep a seat by the sea.
(when you keep a seat by the sea)

[Interlude: Lyrical violin and mandolin]

[Verse 2: Warm male vocal, steady pulse]
Down along the pier where the fishing boats
rock gently on the morning tide,
someone pours the coffee warm and dark
with an extra mug beside.
And they set two chairs against the rail
as the silver gulls go gliding by,
where a neighbor always has a place
underneath the open sky.

[Chorus: Full acoustic band, female harmony]
Keep a seat by the sea for me,
where the morning wind blows free,
where the white sails drift across the bay
at the breaking of the day.
There's an open place for you and me
when you keep a seat by the sea.
(when you keep a seat by the sea)

[Instrumental Solo: Lyrical violin and acoustic guitar]

[Bridge: Intimate vocal, gentle guitar]
No need to ask a stranger's name
or where the winding road has been,
for the bench upon the cliff is wide
and it lets the morning in.

[Final Chorus: Rich male and female harmony]
Keep a seat by the sea for me,
where the daylight shines so free,
where a friendly hand along the way
tells a wandering heart to stay.
There's an open place for you and me
(for you and me)
when you keep a seat by the sea.

[Outro: Fading guitar and soft whistling]
When you keep a seat by the sea,
(mmm-hmm...)
sit a while with me.
```

## Track 2: Where the Evening Water Shines

Hoàng hôn và chạng vạng lắng xuống trên vịnh biển xanh thẫm: qua những bậc thềm đá và cầu tàu lúc chiều muộn, hai chiếc ghế gỗ được xoay sẵn hướng về phía mặt trời lặn để người hàng xóm đi bộ qua không bao giờ phải ngồi ngắm chiều buông một mình, và bát súp nóng được sẻ chia bên bậc đá cảng khi những con thuyền nhỏ cập bến.

### Style prompt
```text
Warm coastal Americana acoustic folk in D major with gentle G-major harmonic lifts, 72 BPM warm evening sway. Flesh-fingerpicked acoustic guitar with natural timing variations leads alongside soft strummed chords. Warm woody upright bass sits slightly behind the beat over a light felt kick and steady brushed snare maintaining a smooth, comforting flow. Lyrical cello and soft felt piano chords weave warm counter-melodies, supported by gentle vocal humming. Mature weathered male baritone and warm female alto trade lines in an intimate close-mic duet, softening in the bridge before blooming into a rich, uplifting final chorus and gentle acoustic outro. Warm low-mid focused analog production with rolled-off gentle highs.
```

### Exclude
```text
EDM, glossy pop, aggressive drums, autotune, synths, harsh high frequencies
```

### Lyrics
```text
[Intro: Fingerpicked guitar, cello, and felt piano]

[Verse 1: Breathy female vocal, close-mic]
The evening sun is sinking low
across the quiet bay,
and leaves a warm and golden glow
to end the gentle day.
[Warm male vocal]
Two wooden chairs upon the stone
are turned toward the sea,
so anyone who walks alone
can sit a while with me.

[Pre-Chorus: Male and female duet]
Now the wind grows soft along the shore,
and no one has to hurry anymore.

[Chorus: Warm male and female harmony]
Come and rest beside the quiet bay,
let the busy hours fade away.
All the boats are tied along the pier,
and the harbor lights are warm and clear.
Sit with me beneath the coastal pines,
where the evening water shines.

[Interlude: Warm cello and felt piano]
(Mmm... where the water shines...)

[Verse 2: Warm male vocal]
Down by the steps along the side
where all the boats come in,
they tie the ropes against the tide
as evening stars begin.
[Female vocal joins in harmony]
A steaming bowl is passed by hand
to warm the harbor chill,
as quiet settles on the land
and all the waves grow still.

[Pre-Chorus: Male and female duet]
Now the first pale star begins to rise
across the quiet of the evening skies.

[Chorus: Warm male and female harmony]
Come and rest beside the quiet bay,
let the busy hours fade away.
All the boats are tied along the pier,
and the harbor lights are warm and clear.
Sit with me beneath the coastal pines,
where the evening water shines.

[Instrumental Solo: Solo cello and felt piano]

[Bridge: Hushed guitar breakdown, trading lines]
[Female vocal]
The waves roll in and softly go
[Male vocal]
against the harbor stone,
[Both, tender harmony]
and in the twilight's gentle glow,
you never sit alone.

[Final Chorus: Hushed opening, warm string bloom]
Come and rest beside the quiet bay,
let the busy hours fade away.
Two old chairs are waiting on the pier,
and the harbor lights are warm and clear.
Side by side beneath the coastal pines,
(beneath the pines)
where the evening water shines.

[Outro: Fading acoustic guitar and warm cello]
Where the evening water shines,
(mmm-hmm...)
sit a while with me.
```
