## MÔ TẢ

Hello, my dear friends,

For a long time, we spend our lives pulling hard against the oars, convinced that safety only exists when we reach another solid shore. We build heavy walls around ourselves just to stay dry, and in the process, we forget to look up at the wide open sky.

This song, "Tide and Timber," was born from a simple realization on a quiet morning by the water: the tide does not hurry to the sand, and the sea doesn't ask you to carry your burdens. Sometimes, the most courageous thing you can do is let go of the oars, breathe, and let the deep water hold you.

In this handcrafted diorama film, you will follow the gentle rhythms of an island sanctuary—weathered fishermen carving cedar dories, islanders gathering salt along misty reefs, and solitary boats drifting into the endless golden horizon. Accompanying their quiet life is an original acoustic indie folk piece with fingerpicked guitar, warm cello swells, and the natural pulse of ocean waves.

I hope this music gives you a safe harbor today, a moment to set down whatever you've been carrying, and a reminder that you are allowed to rest. Thank you so much for being here with me.
