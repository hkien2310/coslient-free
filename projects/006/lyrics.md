# PROJECT 006: CLEAN ENGLISH LYRICS : "BORROW NO WINGS"
*Thư mục: `projects/006/lyrics.md` | Ngày cập nhật: 17/08/2026*

---

## 1. PURE CLEAN LYRICS (FULL VERSION : ZERO TAGS)

```text
Drop the load...
Walk slow...

Iron straps around the chest,
Told to fly above the trees.
Now I sit among the rest,
With my feet in morning breeze.

Left the metal on the stone,
Left the hurry in the cold.
Just a boy of pine and bone,
Watching summer turn to gold.

Oh, borrow no wings today,
Let your own two feet touch ground.
All the running goes away,
When the quiet path is found.

You were never made of steel,
You were made to walk and breathe.
Feel the dust upon the heel,
Feel the grass under your sleeve.

Drop the straps into the moss...
Watch the clouds go rolling by...
Nothing broken, nothing lost...
You don't even have to fly.

Just walk...
The path is wide.
```
