## 📜 3. SUNO PERFORMANCE LYRICS (BẢN TẠO NHẠC — 100% TỪ 1–2 ÂM TIẾT, BẢN SẮC 017)

```text
# The Gentle Road

[Intro | Hypnotic circular fingerpicked acoustic guitar, repetitive warm upright piano ostinato, gentle soft whistling]

[Verse 1 | Intimate breathy vocals, quiet fingerpicking, soft upright bass]
The postman rides his heavy bike along the dusty lane
A sparrow perches on the bar through morning sun and rain
The baker slides a tray of crusts along the wooden sill
The warm bread scents the quiet air that wanders down the hill

[Chorus 1 | Whispered duet harmonies, rolling acoustic lullaby groove, sweet melodic whistling]
Step by step slow and sweet the white geese cross the road
The wagon stops the neighbor waits to share a lighter load
A forest fawn eats from the hand beneath the apple bough
There is no hurry in this town the world is gentle now

[Interlude | Mesmerizing cyclic piano and acoustic guitar groove, playful whistling]

[Verse 2 | Warm close-mic vocal, gentle fingerpicked guitar, light brushed percussion]
The gray-haired woman tends her peas and hums an easy tune
The wild deer steps from timber shade to greet the sunny noon
Two neighbors lean across the rail to pass a bowl of plums
A tabby cat rubs on their boots and purrs as midday comes

[Chorus 2 | Warm duet harmonies, swelling acoustic layers, rolling rhythm]
Step by step slow and sweet the white geese cross the road
The wagon stops the neighbor waits to share a lighter load
A forest fawn eats from the hand beneath the apple bough
There is no hurry in this town the world is gentle now

[Bridge | Strip down to intimate fingerpicking, warm upright bass, whispered harmonies]
The mountain water runs so cold across the river stone
No creature walks along these paths to ever feel alone
We leave the latch undone at night the windows open wide
To let the pine wind cool the room where quiet hearts abide

[Solo | Soulful floating acoustic guitar and whistling melodic ostinato over warm upright bass]

[Final Chorus | Dynamic acoustic peak, lush vocal harmonies, rich soothing groove]
Step by step slow and sweet the white geese cross the road
The wagon stops the neighbor waits to share a lighter load
A forest fawn eats from the hand beneath the apple bough
There is no hurry in this town the world is gentle now

[Outro | Gentle rocking cradle tempo, soft fading piano notes, breathy outro vocal]
Blue smoke curls up into the dusk from every chimney clay
The tired horses turn to rest and dream the night away
The yellow windows light the trees like honey in the comb
And every quiet working soul is settled and at home

[Fade Out | Final warm piano chord ringing into silence]
```
