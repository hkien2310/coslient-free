# 022: Đi Chậm Cùng Nhau

Hai bài cùng một style. Người kể là ông chăn cừu, nói với con chó già (con cái, gọi là "girl").

---

## Track 1: The Sheep Stand Still

Buổi sáng lùa đàn lên đồi.

### Style prompt
```text
Scottish Borders folk ballad. Unhurried walking pocket, 68 BPM, major key. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar lead, fiddle, tin whistle, upright bass. Warm weathered male baritone, conversational delivery, clear diction. Gentle verses swelling into a warm chorus. Warm analog tape room, dry acoustic space.
```

### Exclude
```text
autotune, synths, electronic drums, vocal ad-libs, bagpipes
```

### Lyrics
```text
[Intro | Long instrumental intro, fingerpicked guitar, tin whistle melody, morning birds, unhurried]

[Verse 1 | Warm storytelling vocal, fingerpicked guitar, light bass]
You beat me to the gate again,
frost still sitting on your back.
I'm fumbling with my second glove,
you're staring up the track.
Then here they come out of the barn,
four pups and not a lick of sense.
One's got my cuff between his teeth,
one goes headfirst through the fence.

[Pre-Chorus | Fiddle enters softly]
So we start up through the bracken,
the bell out front, the wind behind.
The pups yap at the thistles,
you don't pay them any mind.

[Chorus | Guitar opens to strumming, fiddle carries the melody]
The young ones tear off up the hill,
you stay right here by me.
Halfway up I stop for breath,
and the sheep stand still and wait for me.

[Interlude | Tin whistle and fiddle trade the melody]

[Verse 2 | Intimate vocal, guitar and upright bass]
The pups go charging at the ewes
and scatter them across the slope.
You don't bark, you just stand up,
and back they come, nice and slow.
There's a lamb I bottle-fed in March,
she still comes trotting when I call.
She shoves her nose in my pocket,
like I owe her a crust, that's all.

[Pre-Chorus | Fiddle enters softly]
So we start up through the bracken,
the bell out front, the wind behind.
The pups yap at the thistles,
you don't pay them any mind.

[Chorus | Fuller, warm harmony on the last line]
The young ones tear off up the hill,
you stay right here by me.
Halfway up I stop for breath,
and the sheep stand still and wait for me.

[Fiddle Solo | Warm and unhurried, guitar underneath]

[Bridge | Stripped to vocal and single guitar, tender and quiet]
I taught you all of this, old girl,
back when you chewed my laces through.
Now I take the stones one by one,
and you take them with me too.
You could run up with the young ones,
you've still got the legs, I've seen.
But you look back down for me,
and you settle in between.

[Tin Whistle Solo | Gentle, band builds back in underneath]

[Chorus | Full band returns gently]
The young ones tear off up the hill,
you stay right here by me.
Halfway up I stop for breath,
and the sheep stand still and wait for me.

[Final Chorus | Fiddle and tin whistle together, warm and settled]
The young ones flop down in the grass,
you lean your weight on me.
I sit a minute on the wall,
and the sheep stand still and wait for me.

[Outro | Fiddle and tin whistle play the chorus melody once through, morning birds return]
The sheep stand still and wait for me.
Good girl.
```

## Track 2: On My Left Side

Buổi chiều đưa đàn về. Giữ style Track 1, chỉ đổi BPM và đường cảm xúc.

### Style prompt
```text
Scottish Borders folk ballad. Unhurried walking pocket, 64 BPM, major key. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar lead, fiddle, tin whistle, upright bass. Warm weathered male baritone, conversational delivery, clear diction. Stripped-back reflective storytelling, tender lifts. Warm analog tape room, dry acoustic space.
```

### Exclude
```text
autotune, synths, electronic drums, vocal ad-libs, bagpipes
```

### Lyrics
```text
[Intro | Long instrumental intro, fingerpicked guitar, soft fiddle, evening wind, unhurried]

[Verse 1 | Warm storytelling vocal, fingerpicked guitar, light bass]
The sun's gone low along the wall,
our shadows stretch out down the hill.
The pups have the flock in a ring,
crooked as a dog's hind leg, but still.
One swings too wide, one cuts too close,
one quits to dig a rabbit hole.
I have to laugh, I was the same
my first spring out on my own.

[Pre-Chorus | Fiddle enters softly]
So I whistle once and let them be,
the bell keeps swinging down the track.
One pup runs back to check on you,
then off he goes, don't look back.

[Chorus | Guitar opens to strumming, fiddle carries the melody]
You don't run the edges anymore,
you've earned that, girl, and so have I.
We take the gravel step by step,
and you walk on my left side.

[Interlude | Tin whistle and fiddle, evening wind]

[Verse 2 | Intimate vocal, guitar and upright bass]
Your nose bumps up against my glove,
cold and wet, like every day.
You're checking that I'm still back here.
I am. Go on. I'm on my way.
The bell goes quiet at the bend,
the old ewe's stopped there on the slope.
She waits till she can see us both,
then takes the flock on down the road.

[Pre-Chorus | Fiddle enters softly]
So I whistle once and let them be,
the bell keeps swinging down the track.
One pup runs back to check on you,
then off he goes, don't look back.

[Chorus | Fuller, warm harmony on the last line]
You don't run the edges anymore,
you've earned that, girl, and so have I.
We take the gravel step by step,
and you walk on my left side.

[Fiddle Solo | Low and tender, guitar underneath]

[Bridge | Stripped to vocal and single guitar, tender and quiet]
Can't say how many years we've got,
your nose is grey, I'm grey all through.
But the pups are coming on just fine,
no rush tonight for me or you.
Some nights you're curled up by the stove,
your feet go running in a dream.
I'd like to think it's this same hill,
with the bell and the gate and me.

[Tin Whistle Solo | Gentle, band builds back in underneath]

[Chorus | Full band returns gently]
You don't run the edges anymore,
you've earned that, girl, and so have I.
We take the gravel step by step,
and you walk on my left side.

[Final Chorus | Fiddle and tin whistle together, warm and settled]
Forty-two in, and all four pups,
the kitchen light's on up the rise.
I swing the old gate shut behind,
and you walk on my left side.

[Outro | Fiddle and tin whistle play the chorus melody once through, evening wind fades]
You walk on my left side.
Come on, girl.
```
