# 024: Hiệp Sĩ Bìa Các-Tông Và Những Hiên Nhà Thắp Sáng

Hai bài hát phong cách Warm Acoustic Folk / Americana ở tông G trưởng (G major), áp dụng kỹ thuật **Hiện tại quen thuộc & Tín hiệu gợi mở (Familiar Present & Subtle Cues)** của [`craft/music_craft.md`](../../craft/music_craft.md):
- **Phần Lời & Nhạc (`2-music.md`):** Đặt người hát vào khoảnh khắc hiện tại ngoài hiên nhà (`same old porch`, `still`, `like back then`), gợi câu chuyện bằng những tín hiệu gián tiếp (`little cape beside the door`, `small steps tapping on the floor`, `paper crown`, `candle glowing on the step`, `kids still laughing down the lane`) kết hợp điệp khúc tâm tình mở (*You / We*).
- **Phần Hình ảnh (`storyboard.md`):** Đảm nhận việc diễn giải trực diện hoạt cảnh ông bà cắt thùng giấy làm giáp hiệp sĩ, bọc giấy bạc lên khiên và dẫn cháu đi qua từng hiên nhà hàng xóm đêm Halloween.

---

## Track 1: The Cardboard Knight

Buổi chiều nắng thu chậm lại trên bậc thềm gỗ quen thuộc: nghe tiếng bước chân nhỏ chạy qua sàn, nhìn chiếc áo choàng nhỏ bên cửa và chiếc vương miện giấy hơi lệch, người hát mỉm cười nhận ra mình đã đi qua những năm tháng vội vã để có trọn vẹn ngày hôm nay bên người mình thương.

### Style prompt
```text
Warm cinematic acoustic folk with Americana and subtle country soul influence in G major, 76 BPM. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar leads with natural timing variations, supported by soft strummed layers creating a gentle forward motion. Groove is steady and grounded with light kick and brushed snare, maintaining a smooth flow. Upright bass sits slightly behind the beat, adding warmth and weight. Sparse piano chords, distant harmonica phrases, and a light mandolin melodic hook add space and nostalgia, with faint wooden porch steps blended subtly into the rhythm. Arrangement builds gradually with stronger choruses for memorability while remaining restrained. Vocals are mature male, warm, slightly weathered, intimate and close, calm and content, with soft female harmonies in choruses and a simple, hummable melody.
```

### Exclude
```text
EDM, glossy pop, aggressive drums, autotune, synths, electronic drums
```

### Lyrics
```text
[Intro] (soft autumn breeze, faint wooden porch steps) (fingerpicked acoustic guitar with natural timing variations, light mandolin hook)

[Verse 1][Gentle fingerpicked acoustic guitar starts. The mature male voice enters, warm and close]
Afternoon falls a little slower now,
same old porch, I sit right down.
Sun leans warm against the stair,
like it always did back then.
A little cape beside the door,
small steps tapping on the floor.
Before I even say a word,
you reach and take my hand.

[Pre-Chorus]
Used to rush through every day,
now I got the time to stay.

[Chorus][Music swells warmly with brushed snare, upright bass, and soft female harmonies]
You take your time, I'll take mine too,
every step I walk with you.
I spent too long running fast,
missing things that couldn't last.
Now the sun is warm and low,
you take your time, I'll take it slow.

[Interlude] (distant harmonica, sparse piano chords, and fingerpicked guitar over steady brushed snare)

[Verse 2][Low upright bass and soft brushed snare maintain a slow, steady pulse]
Days don't run like they used to do,
I let the afternoon pass through.
Used to watch the kitchen clock,
always running out of light.
That paper crown, that crooked fold,
a small, familiar thing to hold.
Funny how the smallest laugh
can set the whole world right.

[Pre-Chorus]
No need to ask where we should go,
you already seem to know.

[Chorus][Music swells warmly, soft female harmonies blend in]
You take your time, I'll take mine too,
every step I walk with you.
I spent too long running fast,
missing things that couldn't last.
Now the sun is warm and low,
you take your time, I'll take it slow.

[Break] (warm harmonica, sparse piano notes, and gentle acoustic guitar strumming)

[Bridge][Instruments soften, intimate and restrained delivery]
I've had the years come and go,
this quiet step is all I need to know.

[Chorus][Warm acoustic band lifts gently with rich harmonies]
You take your time, I'll take mine too,
every step I walk with you.
I spent too long running fast,
missing things that couldn't last.
Now the sun is warm and low,
you take your time, I'll take it slow.

[Final Chorus][Full emotional warmth, restrained and tender]
You take your time, I'll take mine too,
no other place I'd rather be with you.
I don't count what's left behind,
every hour has been so kind.
Now the sun is warm and low,
(take it slow)
you take your time, I'll take it slow.

[Outro][Music fades back to soft fingerpicked guitar]
Same old porch, same small hand,
still right here.
```

## Track 2: Leave the Porch Light On

Chập tối tháng Mười lắng xuống trên con đường cũ: ngọn nến vàng ấm vừa thắp lên trên bậc thềm, tiếng trẻ con cười nói dọc ngõ nhỏ như bao mùa thu trước, và ánh đèn hiên nhà luôn để sáng đợi người thương trở về.

### Style prompt
```text
Warm acoustic folk with subtle Americana influence in G major, 74 BPM. Fingerpicked acoustic guitar forms the foundation, slightly imperfect for a natural human feel, layered with gentle strummed accents for warmth. Minimal rhythm with soft kick pulse and brushed snare, creating a slow, steady sway. Light upright bass sits slightly behind the beat. Sweeping cello pads, occasional piano notes, and soft harmonica textures enhance nostalgia, with faint wind chimes blended subtly into the arrangement. Arrangement builds gradually with stronger choruses while remaining restrained. Vocals are mature male, warm, slightly weathered, intimate and close, with natural phrasing, soft female harmonies in choruses, and a simple, hummable, soothing melody. Clean, organic, timeless production.
```

### Exclude
```text
EDM, glossy pop, aggressive drums, autotune, synths, electronic drums
```

### Lyrics
```text
[Intro] (faint wind chimes, soft dry leaves) (gentle fingerpicked acoustic guitar and warm cello pad)

[Verse 1][Gentle fingerpicked acoustic guitar starts. The mature male voice enters, warm and close]
Evening settles a little slower now,
same old lane, we walk on down.
Dry leaves rustle by the gate,
like they always did back then.
Candle glowing on the step,
that same warm light we always kept.
Then I hear it down the hill,
that little laugh again.

[Pre-Chorus]
No matter how the seasons roll,
some small things don't grow old.

[Chorus][Music swells gently with brushed snare, upright bass, and soft female harmonies]
Leave the porch light on tonight,
let it shine across the yard.
We have walked a winding road,
some of it was long and hard.
Now we step into the glow,
leave the porch light on, we're home.

[Interlude] (soft harmonica, occasional piano notes, and fingerpicked guitar over a gentle sway)

[Verse 2][Sweeping cello pads and soft brushed snare maintain a slow, grounded pulse]
Kids still laughing down the street,
soft steps passing by the tree.
Nothing here runs all too fast,
that's why the good things stay.
A wooden chair beside the door,
same friendly wave we knew before.
You lean your head against my coat
at the closing of the day.

[Pre-Chorus]
They don't count the passing time,
just a warm hand holding mine.

[Chorus][Music swells gently, soft female harmonies blend in]
Leave the porch light on tonight,
let it shine across the yard.
We have walked a winding road,
some of it was long and hard.
Now we step into the glow,
leave the porch light on, we're home.

[Break] (distant harmonica, sweeping cello, and gentle piano chords)

[Bridge][Instruments soften, intimate storytelling delivery]
I don't need much these days,
just a quiet light that always stays.

[Chorus][Warm acoustic lift with rich harmonies]
Leave the porch light on tonight,
let it shine across the yard.
We have walked a winding road,
some of it was long and hard.
Now we step into the glow,
leave the porch light on, we're home.

[Final Chorus][Tender, deep emotional warmth, restrained delivery]
Leave the porch light on tonight,
keep it burning warm and clear.
Every mile I ever walked
was leading me right here.
Now we step into the glow,
(we are home)
leave the porch light on, we're home.

[Outro][Music fades back to just the soft fingerpicked guitar]
Same warm light, same quiet door,
still around.
```
